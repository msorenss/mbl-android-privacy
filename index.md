# MBL Android Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

MBL Android is an Android client for Media Box Lounge, published by Markus Robert Sörensson. It connects to the MBL server you choose. The operator of that server controls its accounts, libraries, logs and retention settings.

## Information the app uses

- **Connection and sign-in.** The app stores your server address and a session token on your device. When you sign in, it sends your username, password, and any required verification code to your selected server. It also sends a client name and device model so the server can identify the session.
- **Media and activity.** The app exchanges library searches, playback requests, watch progress, playlist changes and media requests with the selected server as needed for the features you use. Your server may keep a record of this activity.
- **On-device data.** The app keeps preferences, offline downloads and pending offline watch progress on your device. When a connection becomes available, pending watch progress can sync to your server.
- **Optional features.** Local discovery looks for MBL servers on your network. If you cast, the selected Google Cast receiver receives the information needed to play the stream. WebTV can send searches and playback requests to the selected MBL server, which may use external sources. If you open an external source, that site's privacy policy applies.

## Retention and deletion

You can remove offline videos in Downloads and clear app data in Android settings to delete local app data. Signing out clears the saved session from the app. Clearing local data does not delete your MBL server account or history. Contact your server operator for access to or deletion of server-side data and for details about their retention settings.
